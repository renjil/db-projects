### Business Definitions

- "Revenue" means: SUM(products.price * orders.quantity - orders.discount_applied)
- "Average order value" or "AOV" means: total revenue divided by count of distinct orders
- "Active customer" means: a customer who placed at least one order in the last 30 days
- "Best-selling" refers to quantity sold unless the user specifies "by revenue"

### Time Periods

- "This month" refers to April 2024 (our most recent data)
- "Last month" refers to March 2024
- When users ask about "recent" orders, default to the last 30 days
- Our fiscal year starts January 1

### Product Categories

- "Wellness shots" are small-sized, low-calorie health drinks (Immunity Booster, Energy Shot)
- "Seasonal products" include Pumpkin Spice Dream (fall) and Watermelon Wave (summer)
- When asked about "healthy options", prioritize organic products and those under 150 calories

### Customer Segments

- "VIP customers" means Gold or Platinum tier members
- "New customers" means customers who signed up in the last 90 days
- Customer lifetime value should be calculated as total revenue from all their orders

### Store Operations

- Morning shift: orders before 12:00
- Afternoon shift: orders 12:00-17:00
- Evening shift: orders after 17:00
- Our busiest store is typically Downtown

### Common Calculations

- When showing percentages, round to 1 decimal place
- When showing currency, format as USD with 2 decimal places
- For rankings, show top 5 by default unless specified
